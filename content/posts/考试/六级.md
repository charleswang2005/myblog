## 作文模板
### 优秀品质技能类
With the enhancement of general competitiveness in China, people nowadays are increasingly aware of the importance of young people/ social harmony. And xx plays a crucial role in their/its development.
随着中国整体竞争力不断提升，现在大家越来越意识到年轻人／社会和谐有多重要。而 xx 在他们／它的发展过程中起着关键作用。
To examine the significance of xx, certain factors could be specifically pointed out here. First and foremost, xx will facilitate ① our performance in relevant aspects ② win-win cooperation (between A and B). Another factor that deserves to be cited here is that ① people of xx/ ② xx could boost ① the overall productivity of our society ② the communication efficiency and the overall effectiveness of .... Last but not the least, if we/A and B fail to ① recognize the importance of xx ② do sth, there would eventually be a heavy toll to pay sooner or later.
要说清 xx 为什么重要，可以具体列出这么几点。首先，xx 能①帮我们在相关方面表现得更好，②促成（A和B之间的）双赢合作。还有一点值得提：①具备 xx 的人／② xx 本身，能①提高整个社会的生产率，②提升……的沟通效率和整体效果。最后同样重要的是，如果我们／A和B没做到①认识到 xx 的重要性／②去做某件事，迟早是要付出大代价的。
In brief, xx is of true essentiality and concrete measures must be taken to secure it. Therefore, ① governments should implement proper policies and launch mass media campaigns ② schools should provide open courses to infuse such an indispensable virtue/awareness/skill into everyone. The more rapidly actions are taken, the more effectively this virtue/awareness/skill will benefit us in the upcoming future.
总之，xx 确实必不可少，得拿出实实在在的办法来保住它。所以，①政府该出台合适的政策、借大众媒体做宣传，②学校该开公开课，把这种少不了的品质／意识／技能灌进每个人脑子里。行动得越快，这种品质／意识／技能在今后的日子里就越能早点帮到我们。
### 选择平衡类模板（熟读，不强求背诵）
It is universally acknowledged that, all other factors being equal, a proper ① choice on sth ② balance between A and B could be the difference between one's success or failure. But when it comes to ① whether to choose A or B ② how to keep a balance between A and B, the answer may vary from person to person.
大家都公认：在其他条件都一样的情况下，一个妥当的①关于某事的选择／② A和B之间的平衡，就能决定一个人是成还是败。可一旦落到①到底选A还是选B／②怎么在A和B之间保持平衡，答案就因人而异了。
To make an advisable choice/balance between A and B, we should carefully examine in which way are they beneficial for us. Firstly, A.... By contrast, B....（选择）From my perspective, those that are fit for you are the best. We should choose our xx respectively according to our interests and goals.（平衡）From my perspective, we should reasonably allocate our energy and make flexible adjustment to maximize ① the overall efficiency of work/study ② the overall quality of our life.
要想在A和B之间做出靠谱的选择／平衡，得先仔细捋一捋它们各自对我们好在哪儿。先说A……；反过来，B……。（走「选择」这条）依我看，适合你的才是最好的。我们该按自己的兴趣和目标，各人挑各人的 xx。（走「平衡」这条）依我看，该把精力合理分配、再灵活调整，好让①工作／学习的整体效率、②生活的整体质量都拉到最高。
In addition, considering its significance, public supports should be offered. For instance, ① governments could launch mass media campaigns ② schools could provide open courses to provide the relevant knowledge and guidance, which will be indispensable for people to reach a better choice/balance between A and B.
另外，这事既然这么重要，外界也该搭把手。比如，①政府可以用大众媒体做宣传，②学校可以开公开课，把相关的知识和指导给到大家——这些东西对人们做出更好的选择／拿捏好A和B的平衡，是少不了的。
